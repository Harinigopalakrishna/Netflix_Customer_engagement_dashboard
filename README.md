Netflix Customer Engagement Analysis

Project Overview:

This project analyzes Netflix customer engagement and content performance using Power BI and Excel. The analysis focuses on customer behavior, watch time, subscription types, content preferences, ratings, genres, and country-wise engagement.
The goal is to convert raw Netflix customer and content data into meaningful business insights that can support content planning, customer engagement, and business decision-making.

Objectives:

* Analyze customer engagement across different age groups
* Compare watch time across subscription types
* Identify the most-watched content
* Analyze popular genres and content types
* Compare Movies and TV Shows
* Analyze customer ratings
* Identify recently added and trending content
* Analyze customer retention and viewing behavior
* Compare country-wise engagement and ratings
* Analyze director performance and content growth over time

Datase: The project uses two datasets:

Netflix Customer Ratings Dataset – contains customer-level information such as customer ID, age, subscription type, watch time, country, genre, ratings, and content type.
Netflix Content Dataset – contains information about Netflix movies and TV shows, including title, genre, country, director, cast, type, and date added.


 Tools & Technologies:

* Microsoft Excel – Data preparation and analysis
* Power BI – Data visualization and dashboard development
* DAX – Measures and calculated analysis
  
 Key Insights:

* 20K customers were analyzed.
* Total watch time was approximately 835.21K hours.
* Average watch time per customer was approximately 2.51K minutes.
* The overall average customer rating was 3.01/5.
* The 60+ age group recorded the highest watch time at approximately 245K hours.
* The Standard subscription had the highest customer share at 34.46%.
* Movies represented 50.49% of the observed content preference, while TV Shows represented **49.51%**.
* Romance recorded approximately 89K watch hours among the highlighted genres.
* October recorded the highest monthly watch volume at approximately 77K hours.
* July recorded the highest monthly average rating at 3.08/5.

Dashboard:

The Power BI dashboard provides an interactive view of Netflix customer engagement using:

* KPI cards
* Age-group analysis
* Subscription analysis
* Watch-time analysis
* Movie vs TV Show comparison
* Genre analysis
* Rating analysis
* Country analysis
* Interactive slicers

-- > Users can filter the dashboard by subscription type, country, age group, content type, and date to explore specific customer and content segments.

Conclusion:

This project provides a clear view of **Netflix customer engagement, content consumption, ratings, and subscription patterns**. Power BI helps bring these insights together through interactive visualizations and dashboards, supporting better understanding of customer behavior and content performance.
